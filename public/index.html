The root cause

Every CRM was built for the wrong person.

Every CRM on the market was designed for a VP of Sales with a team, an admin, and a defined process. When solo founders try to use them, the same breakdown happens every time. Not because of poor discipline. Because of architecture.

1
CRM asks you to define your pipeline stages

You don't know your process yet. You're still figuring it out.

2
Every conversation requires manual logging

Logging competes with actually selling. One always loses.

3
Logging fatigue sets in within days

Data gets incomplete. The CRM becomes a lie.

4
CRM is abandoned by week 6

You're back to WhatsApp, memory, and a spreadsheet you'll never update.

5
Deals die in the follow-up gap

No system means no reminders. Warm leads go cold silently. 44% of founders follow up only once, then stop.

This pattern was identical across all 16 CRMs we evaluated, regardless of price, UX, or positioning. The problem isn't the tool. It's the input model.
